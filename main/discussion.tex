\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

APT-Bench reveals a coarse--fine granularity gap without a significant
\ourmethod{} advantage over matched controls. Disease scores are
centering-sensitive, and compact panels establish within-cohort measurement
efficiency rather than causal biomarkers. This single-site, 40-patient cohort
lacks batch metadata and external validation, so novelty remains exploratory.
Its reusable contribution is the fixed schema, splits, tasks, and evaluator
\citep{luecken2025open,liu2025multitask}; clinical claims require cross-site
cohorts.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

APT-Bench defines patient-disjoint hierarchy typing, disease audit, and
measurement-efficiency tasks for single-cell APT profiles. External cohorts
remain necessary for clinical claims.
