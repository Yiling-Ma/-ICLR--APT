\section{Benchmark Reference Models}
\label{sec:method_summary}

\paragraph{APT-only comparisons.}
LR and XGBoost provide linear and nonlinear tabular references. Plain MLP,
Flat and HCE Transformers, and Soft-cascade provide neural references.
The unified v2 main comparison uses raw-count log1p inputs, training-only
standardization, the same 24/8 inner training/validation patients, and fresh
refits on all 32 development patients. All available annotated cells are used.
Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix:unified_v2} specifies model-specific search grids,
exact fold counts, three randomized refit seeds and realized budgets.
LR is deterministic; LR/XGBoost select coarse and fine tasks independently,
whereas neural coarse outputs accompany fine-selected fits. Common data and
selection rules do not imply identical optimization or compute costs.
Historical Linear SVM, Random Forest and reference-correlation results remain
appendix references, not additional rows of this unified comparison.

\paragraph{Hierarchy-aware reference.}
Soft-cascade embeds aptamer identities and values and combines a global
subtype head with lineage-specific experts weighted by coarse probabilities.
Eq.~\ref{eq:cascade} specifies the routing form; v2 dimensions and objectives
are given in Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix:unified_v2}. Ordinary fine predictions
are not restricted to the predicted parent. Flat bypasses the cascade;
HCE uses a two-level hierarchical loss adaptation
\citep{cultrera2026improving}, with parent probabilities decoded from subtree
mass. No main-table model uses disease supervision. A separate $2\times2$
comparison adds the same cross-disease contrastive term to Flat and Cascade.
It tests the whole cascade structure and added supervision under a shared
search protocol, not individual routing components. The earlier architecture,
loss and component matrix in Appendix~\ref{sec:method} describe historical
references and should not be substituted for v2 settings.

\paragraph{Input compatibility.}
Gene-based pretrained models such as scGPT and Geneformer
\citep{cui2024scgpt,theodoris2023transfer} do not directly accept anonymous
aptamer feature IDs without a verified mapping or a separately trained adapter.
Supplying paired RNA would change the input contract. This distinguishes an
undefined direct APT input from an adaptable but unevaluated model; it does
not justify claiming superiority over transcriptomic annotation methods.

\paragraph{Matched-input diagnostics.}
A separate matched-input probe varies APT and RNA while holding its training
budget and patient folds fixed. Training-only subtype priors and true-lineage
masking provide candidate-set controls. APT shuffling within patient, or within
patient and true lineage, is a privileged diagnostic rather than a deployable
model input (Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix:identity_questions}).
RNA also supplies the reference annotations, so this is an internal
reconstruction comparison, not independent label validation. HVG selection
and scaling are fitted separately within each inner and outer training
partition. Table~\ref{tab:identity_training_config} gives input transforms,
training weights, optimization settings and selection grids.
The v2 APT-only hierarchy-signal follow-up instead keeps the full raw-count
APT budget and asks whether true-lineage oracle performance survives a
patient--lineage shuffle control. It also tests a trained conditional subtype
MLP, separating cell-level diagnostic signal from deployable prediction.
The existing provided-expression MLP oracle/prior and failure analyses are
retained as historical supporting diagnostics; they do not use the v2 MLP fits.

\paragraph{Targeted hierarchy-bottleneck mitigation.}
We additionally test whether the diagnosed oracle--deployable gap is actionable
without changing input modality or backbone capacity. The hierarchy-bottleneck
mitigation (HBM) student retains
the v2 MLP encoder and fine/coarse heads. If $p_k$ is its fine distribution and
$G_m$ contains the subtypes under lineage $m$, the implied parent distribution
is $a_m=\sum_{k\in G_m}p_k$. Training can add parent-mass NLL
$-\log a_{m^*}$ and symmetric KL agreement between $a$ and the explicit coarse
distribution $q$. A fold-specific privileged teacher receives APT and the true
training lineage, and supplies temperature-scaled soft targets only among
$G_{m^*}$. The student distillation term is
$T^2\mathrm{KL}(p_T(\cdot\mid x,m^*)\Vert
p_S(\cdot\mid x,m^*))$. The teacher is frozen before distillation; the
deployable student receives only APT and predicts from its ordinary 27-way fine
head. Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix:hbm} gives the staged validation grids,
isolation checks and complete diagnostics.
