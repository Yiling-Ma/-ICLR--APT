\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Single-cell aptamer profiling.} Single-cell aptamer studies investigate cell discrimination, biological characterization, and probe discovery \citep{xu2024aptamer,wu2024efficient}. Apt-seq demonstrated paired aptamer and transcriptome measurements and cell discrimination from binding profiles \citep{delley2018combined}. Aptomics extended joint profiling to cell-surface proteins, glycans, and mRNA, examining tumor heterogeneity in clinical samples and glycosylation changes during T-cell differentiation \citep{wu2025high}. SPARK-seq combines CRISPR-based surface-protein perturbations with single-cell mRNA and aptamer sequencing to identify aptamer targets and characterize binding kinetics \citep{luo2026spark}. \ourbench{} builds on these measurement capabilities and biological applications to evaluate fixed-panel APT-based cell typing.

\paragraph{Cell annotation and hierarchical prediction.} CellTypist and SingleR exemplify supervised and reference-based cell annotation \citep{dominguez2022cross,aran2019reference}, while scGPT and Geneformer learn representations from large transcriptomic corpora for downstream fine-tuning \citep{cui2024scgpt,theodoris2023transfer}. CHETAH and scHPL organize reference populations into classification hierarchies to accommodate different annotation resolutions \citep{de2019chetah,michielsen2021hierarchical}. Hierarchical cross-entropy (HCE) incorporates ancestor--descendant label relationships into the training objective \citep{cultrera2026improving}. \ourbench{} evaluates classical and hierarchy-aware reference models; its true-lineage oracle and conditional priors serve as separate diagnostics with access to the same reference lineage.

\paragraph{Multimodal integration and predictive utility.} WNN learns cell-specific modality weights for integrated neighborhood analysis \citep{hao2021integrated}, while MOFA+, totalVI, and MultiVI provide latent-variable frameworks for joint representation learning and downstream analysis \citep{argelaguet2020mofa+,gayoso2021joint,ashuach2023multivi}. \citet{liu2025multitask} benchmarked integration methods across modality and batch configurations on tasks including classification, clustering, batch correction, and imputation. \ourbench{} examines APT's incremental predictive utility by comparing RNA+APT with specified RNA-only references under controlled training settings. Shuffling APT profiles within patients or patient--reference-lineage groups further assesses whether predictive gains depend on the original cell-level pairing beyond the preserved group structure.

\paragraph{Single-cell benchmarking and generalization.} Annotation and integration benchmarks provide reusable workflows for systematic method comparison \citep{abdelaal2019comparison,luecken2022benchmarking}, and Open Problems organizes evaluation around defined tasks, datasets, method interfaces, and metrics \citep{luecken2025defining}. Within-individual cell dependence is documented in studies of pseudoreplication \citep{zimmerman2021practical}, and scTab provides a precedent for donor-wise annotation evaluation \citep{fischer2024sctab}. Targeting cell-identity prediction in new patients, \ourbench{} specifies a fixed APT panel, paired RNA--APT measurements, an RNA-derived reference hierarchy, fixed patient-disjoint splits, and patient-balanced scoring. This setting establishes common reference results for APT-based cell typing, complemented by diagnostics of hierarchical errors and modality pairing.
