\subsection{Reference Performance Under the Patient-Disjoint Contract}
\label{sec:results:subtype_audit}

Table~\ref{tab:main} compares APT-only reference predictions using
subject-balanced pooled Macro-F1 on the same 40 held-out patients.
Coarse/fine scores are 0.298/0.126 for LR and 0.325/0.137 for XGBoost.
Flat, HCE, and Soft-cascade Transformers reach 0.326/0.139,
0.335/0.143, and 0.332/0.145. These point estimates establish reference
performance, not a statistically demonstrated cascade advantage.
The completed full-cell-budget plain MLP reaches 0.334/0.141, averaged over
three training seeds; its joint uncertainty includes training-seed variation.

\subsection{Where Do Hierarchical Typing Errors Occur?}

A privileged-label diagnostic masks each frozen fine score vector to the
children of the true lineage, without retraining or changing routing.
Fine SB-Macro-F1 increases to 0.366/0.361/0.358 for Flat/HCE/Soft-cascade
(Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix:true_lineage_oracle}). This measures the benefit
of knowing the correct parent; it is not deployable performance or evidence
that remaining errors reflect an intrinsic APT information limit.

Narrowing the candidate set can itself improve prediction. We therefore add
training-only subtype priors conditional on the same true lineage to the
three-seed, full-cell-budget MLP diagnostic. Ordinary fine SB-Macro-F1 is
0.141; true-lineage decoding reaches 0.385, versus 0.148 for the conditional
majority-prior decoder and 0.180 for the conditional-proportion expected
confusion baseline. The paired oracle-minus-expected-prior difference is
$+0.205$ (joint 95\% CI $[0.174,0.217]$).
This comparison shows usable within-lineage discrimination beyond these
candidate-set controls under the tested recipe; it is not a measurement of
all biological information in APT.

Figure~\ref{fig:lineage_structure} reports every lineage, including the
heterogeneous \texttt{Other} category. Improvements from ordinary to oracle
decoding differ across lineages: eliminating parent errors is not equally
helpful everywhere. Within-lineage scores also remain heterogeneous. A
matched-budget RNA oracle is needed to contextualize this pattern under the
same label system; that repeated diagnostic is in progress rather than
inferred from RNA scores obtained under another budget.

\subsection{Does Paired APT Add Information Beyond RNA?}

The paired-input diagnostic uses a capped budget, separately from the main
leaderboard. MLP fine SB-Macro-F1 is 0.584 for RNA and 0.606 for RNA+APT
(paired difference $+0.022$; 95\% CI $[0.013,0.037]$).
RNA+APT also exceeds RNA+noise and RNA+within-patient-shuffled APT; the latter
difference is $+0.010$ ($[0.002,0.017]$). The corresponding SGD shuffled
contrast is unresolved. These single-seed controls motivate, but do not settle,
the incremental-information question. A within-patient shuffle destroys both
lineage correspondence and finer cell pairing; its contrast cannot establish
within-lineage subtype complementarity. RNA supplies the annotations, so this is internal
label reconstruction (Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix:paired_rna_oracle}).

The ongoing extension repeats training seeds and shuffles, adds APT
permutation within patient and true lineage, and evaluates all lineages and
RNA-defined difficulty strata. The RNA-only inner-validation margin fixes
the low-confidence threshold before outer evaluation. Test-set RNA errors
are separately labeled retrospective strata, with both correction and harm
reported. These are reference-model difficulties, not intrinsic RNA
ambiguity. Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix:identity_questions} specifies the frozen
controls and the wider-RNA sensitivity gate. No repeated conditional-pairing
or representation-robustness claim is made while these results are pending.

\subsection{A Limited Patient-Composition Case Study}

As a limited aggregation case study, nested subtype summaries reduce mean
absolute reference-composition error from 0.02314 for a training-patient-mean
baseline to 0.01478 (paired reduction 0.00836; 95\% CI
$[0.00648,0.01010]$). This corresponds to mean total variation decreasing
from 0.312 to 0.200, a rescaling of the same error rather than independent
evidence. Lineage error instead changes from 0.05495 to 0.05827, with no
resolved improvement. Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix:composition_recovery}
reports all classes and a direct median-APT ridge control (subtype MAE 0.01421).
The soft-minus-direct difference is unresolved ($+0.00057$;
$[-0.00092,0.00211]$), so aggregation has no demonstrated advantage over
direct patient regression. Disease association
and recorded-QC conditional comparisons are confined to
Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix:disease_patient_level}.
